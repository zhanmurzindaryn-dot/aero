# AeroGuard AI — 90-second pitch

## One sentence
AeroGuard AI is an explainable aviation safety review dashboard that demonstrates how flight, weather, and maintenance signals could be brought together for human review.

## The problem
Safety teams can need to consult multiple sources to understand a potential operational concern. A scattered view can make it harder to see which records deserve attention first and why.

## Our approach
AeroGuard AI organizes sample flight, weather, and maintenance signals in one interface. It ranks illustrative alerts, shows the factors that triggered them, and lets an analyst export a portable report.

## Live demo
- Open Overview and identify the highest sample risk score.
- Open Flight Monitoring and filter by risk band.
- Open Maintenance to inspect sample task cards.
- Open Reports and export JSON/CSV.

## Why it is different
The prototype emphasizes explainability and explicit limitations. It does not pretend that synthetic scores are real predictions, and it keeps the analyst in the loop.

## What comes next
With aviation domain experts, validated data, and appropriate approvals, future work could investigate reliable integrations, audit trails, and carefully evaluated alerting. The current project is a front-end demonstration, not a certified operational system.
